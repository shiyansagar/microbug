Microburg

A pocket-sized city builder. Lay roads, drop lots, and keep people happy. The buildings grow by themselves, from cottages to skyscrapers.

  1. Roads first. Every lot needs a road beside it.
  2. Homes ask for things: a park, shops, jobs, a school. A bubble over a building shows what it is missing.
  3. Four level-4 lots in a 2×2 square merge into one big block, then a tower.
  4. Tap the floating coins, and the balloon when it drifts by, for a bonus.

Drag to move the map. Drag two fingers sideways on a trackpad to turn it (twist two fingers on a phone), or press Q and E. Pinch or scroll to zoom. Zoom right in to see the people on the streets.
